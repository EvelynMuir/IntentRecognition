\section{Closing Statement}

We thank the Associate Editor and all reviewers for a demanding and constructive second round. The two concerns the Associate Editor emphasized, statistical validation and evaluation breadth, drove substantive new work rather than rewriting. We now report five-seed training with confidence intervals and paired $t$-tests against every compared method, together with a cross-dataset study on the Flickr-LDL and Emotion6 label-distribution benchmarks that is placed in the manuscript itself.

Three results are worth stating plainly.

\begin{itemize}
  \item The cross-dataset study reports a significant \emph{degradation} in Clark distance on Flickr-LDL, and on Emotion6 only the Clark-distance improvement is reliable, the very metric FDIL loses on Flickr-LDL. The manuscript states that the two datasets disagree on which metric improves, and confines the claim there to the division of labour between the modules.
  \item The matched LDL-DVS objective stays essentially identical to the baseline under our frozen features, which limits what that comparison can establish.
  \item The significance analysis reports the one contrast that does not reach significance alongside those that do, namely Samples F1 against our own UTD-only ablation.
\end{itemize}

The submitted marked-up manuscript remains double-spaced and single-column and compiles to exactly \res{35 pages}, achieved by condensing repeated protocol text rather than by reducing line spacing. The three supplementary tables that did not fit are reproduced in this letter, alongside the manuscript tables and the case-study figure the reviewers asked about.

We believe the revision now meets the standards the reviewers set, and we are grateful for the care that produced these improvements.
