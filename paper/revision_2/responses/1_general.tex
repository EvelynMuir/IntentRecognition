Two points of context apply throughout, so we state them once here rather than repeating them under each comment.

\rh{The cross-dataset study.} It now spans two axes, and both are in the manuscript. EMOTIC is the larger benchmark ($23{,}571$ images, $34{,}320$ annotated instances) and keeps our multi-label output geometry, but releases multi-annotator labels only for validation and test, so we report it as a controlled cross-validation on that multi-annotated pool rather than as a leaderboard comparison. Flickr-LDL and Emotion6 are smaller yet carry native subjective label distributions under standard published protocols, and they remove the class-wise threshold search entirely. Neither axis alone answers both the scale and the breadth question, which is why we report both.

\rh{What this letter adds.} The manuscript is at the $35$-page limit, so three items live here instead. These are the complete per-method significance statistics (Table~\ref{tab:significance_letter}), the seed dispersion behind the cross-dataset table (Table~\ref{tab:ldl_letter}), and the per-class F1 attribution behind the manuscript's one-sentence interpretability summary (Table~\ref{tab:per_class_attr_letter}). Everything else we describe below is in the revised manuscript, marked in red.
