\section{Closing Statement}

We thank the Associate Editor and all reviewers for a careful and constructive second round. The Associate Editor emphasized two concerns, statistical validation and evaluation breadth. Both drove substantive new work. We now report five-seed training with confidence intervals and Holm-corrected paired $t$-tests against every matched-protocol comparator. We also place a cross-dataset study in the manuscript itself, covering the larger EMOTIC benchmark and the Flickr-LDL and Emotion6 label-distribution benchmarks.

The submitted marked-up manuscript remains double-spaced and single-column and compiles to exactly \res{35 pages}. The three supplementary tables that did not fit are reproduced in this letter, alongside the manuscript tables and the case-study figure the reviewers asked about. We hope the revised manuscript now addresses all concerns.
