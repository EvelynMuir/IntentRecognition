\section{General Response}

We thank the Associate Editor and all reviewers for the careful second-round assessment. Three concerns recurred across the reports. These were the theoretical basis of the functional decoupling, the absence of statistical validation, and evaluation on a single benchmark. Each is answered with new analysis or new experiments in the manuscript rather than with rewriting. The table below maps every concern to the change and its location.

Two points of context apply throughout, so we state them once here rather than repeating them under each comment.

\rh{The cross-dataset study.} We first explored a transfer evaluation on EMOTIC. Its annotation protocol does not naturally match our soft multi-label setting, so we replaced it with Flickr-LDL and Emotion6, which carry native subjective label distributions and are evaluated under standard published protocols. This is why EMOTIC no longer appears in the manuscript.

\rh{What this letter adds.} The manuscript is at the $35$-page limit, so three items live here instead. These are the complete per-method significance statistics (Table~\ref{tab:significance_letter}), the seed dispersion behind the cross-dataset table (Table~\ref{tab:ldl_letter}), and the per-class F1 attribution behind the manuscript's one-sentence interpretability summary (Table~\ref{tab:per_class_attr_letter}). Everything else we describe below is in the revised manuscript, marked in red.
